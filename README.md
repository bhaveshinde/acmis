The Advanced Crypto Market Intelligence System (ACMIS) is an autonomous multi-agent 
framework designed to streamline the end-to-end process of cryptocurrency market 
analysis. By deploying a coordinated squad of specialized agents—including real-time web 
scrapers, NLP-driven sentiment auditors, and machine-learning predictors—the system 
effectively transforms fragmented, high-velocity data into actionable intelligence. ACMIS 
replaces hours of manual research with a high-speed automated pipeline that calculates 
composite risk scores and generates predictive forecasts, ultimately delivering a centralized 
intelligence dashboard that minimizes human bias and maximizes operational efficiency in 
volatile trading environments.
